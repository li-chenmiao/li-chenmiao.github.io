About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of Tokyo. My research focuses on digital currencies, payment systems, and macroeconomics. I study how stablecoins, CBDCs, and digital financial infrastructures affect households’ means-of-payment choices, currency competition, seigniorage, and monetary policy transmission.

Research Interests

Digital currencies; stablecoins; payment systems; monetary economics; macroeconomics; financial inclusion; household consumption.

Working Papers

Stablecoin Entry, Currency Competition, and Monetary Sovereignty
This paper studies how fiat-pegged stablecoins reshape currency competition, seigniorage, and welfare in a Lagos–Wright economy.

Digital Financial Inclusion and Household Consumption Composition
This project studies how digital financial inclusion affects household consumption and its composition in China.
